MODULE 1

Subject & Personal Pronouns

Common Subject Pronouns (Part 1)

I

First Person

Refer to yourself.

Example: I am a student.
You

Second Person

One person or more.

Example: You are my friend.
He

Masculine

A man or boy.

Example: He is a teacher.
She

Feminine

A woman or girl.

Example: She is a nurse.

Common Subject Pronouns (Part 2)

It

Neutral

Things, animals, or places.

Example: It is a backpack.
We

Plural (Incl)

Me and other people.

Example: We are classmates.
They

Plural (Excl)

Other people (no me).

Example: They are friends.

Usage & Quick Tips

Pronoun Meaning Example
I Self (always Capitalized) I study English.
He/She Male / Female He works hard.
It Objects / Situations It is raining.
They Groups (plural) They play soccer.

Quick Tip

Subject pronouns always come before the verb. They are the doers of the action.

MODULE 2

Categorization & Types

Main Pronoun Categories

Personal

Refer to specific persons or things.

Ex: He, she, it, they...

Demonstrative

Used to indicate something specifically.

Ex: This, That, These...

Interrogative

Used when we ask a question.

Ex: Which, What, Whom...

Possessive

Used to indicate ownership of something.

Ex: Yours, Hers, Mine...

Specific Use Categories

Reflexive

End in "-self" or "-selves".

Ex: Myself, Himself...

Relative

Supply more info about a clause.

Ex: Whichever, Whatever...

Indefinite

Refer to unspecified things.

Ex: Anyone, Someone...

Reciprocal

Indicate relationship/task sharing.

Ex: Each other, one another...

MODULE 3

Advanced & Modern Usage

Relative Pronouns Deep Dive

Pronoun Function Example
Who People (Subject) The child who has a blue hat is Canadian.
Whom People (Object) This is the girl whom I talked to.
Which Objects / Animals The dog which sits on the sofa is old.
When Time I miss the times when we were together.
Where Places This is the hotel where we are going.
