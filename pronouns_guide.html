MODULE 1

Subject & Personal Pronouns

Common Subject Pronouns (Part 1)

I

First Person

Refer to yourself.

Example: I am a student.
You

Second Person

One person or more.

Example: You are my friend.
He

Masculine

A man or boy.

Example: He is a teacher.
She

Feminine

A woman or girl.

Example: She is a nurse.

Common Subject Pronouns (Part 2)

It

Neutral

Things, animals, or places.

Example: It is a backpack.
We

Plural (Incl)

Me and other people.

Example: We are classmates.
They

Plural (Excl)

Other people (no me).

Example: They are friends.

Usage & Quick Tips

Pronoun Meaning Example
I Self (always Capitalized) I study English.
He/She Male / Female He works hard.
It Objects / Situations It is raining.
They Groups (plural) They play soccer.
