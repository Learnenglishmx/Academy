MODULE 1

Subject & Personal Pronouns

Common Subject Pronouns (Part 1)

I

First Person

Refer to yourself.
Example: I am a student.

You

Second Person

One person or more.
Example: You are my friend.

He

Masculine

A man or boy.
Example: He is a teacher.

She

Feminine

A woman or girl.
Example: She is a nurse.

Common Subject Pronouns (Part 2)

It

Neutral

Things, animals, or places.
Example: It is a backpack.

We

Plural (Incl)

Me and other people.
Example: We are classmates.

They

Plural (Excl)

Other people (no me).
Example: They are friends.

Usage & Quick Tips

Pronoun Meaning Example
ISelf (always Capitalized)I study English.
He/SheMale / FemaleHe works hard.
ItObjects / SituationsIt is raining.
TheyGroups (plural)They play soccer.

Quick Tip

Subject pronouns always come before the verb. They are the doers of the action.

MODULE 2

Categorization & Types

Main Pronoun Categories

Personal

Refer to specific persons or things.
Ex: He, she, it, they...

Demonstrative

Used to indicate something specifically.
Ex: This, That, These...

Interrogative

Used when we ask a question.
Ex: Which, What, Whom...

Possessive

Used to indicate ownership of something.
Ex: Yours, Hers, Mine...

Specific Use Categories

Reflexive

End in "-self" or "-selves".
Ex: Myself, Himself...

Relative

Supply more info about a clause.
Ex: Whichever, Whatever...

Indefinite

Refer to unspecified things.
Ex: Anyone, Someone...

Reciprocal

Indicate relationship/task sharing.
Ex: Each other, one another...
