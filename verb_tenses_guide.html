TIP: Conjugation

Every tense uses a specific auxiliary.

Cada tiempo usa un auxiliar específico (do, be, have, will).

Present Tenses (Tiempos de Presente)

Tense Structure Example & Translation
Simple S + V(s/es) I write books.
Yo escribo libros.
Continuous am/is/are + V-ing I am writing a book.
Estoy escribiendo un libro.
Perfect have/has + V3 I have written a book.
He escrito un libro.
Perfect Cont. have/has been + V-ing I have been writing a book.
He estado escribiendo un libro.

Past Tenses (Tiempos de Pasado)

Tense Structure Example & Translation
Simple S + V-ed / Irregular I wrote a book yesterday.
Escribí un libro ayer.
Continuous was/were + V-ing I was writing a book all day.
Estaba escribiendo un libro todo el día.
Perfect had + V3 I had written a book.
Yo había escrito un libro.
Perfect Cont. had + been + V-ing I had been writing for hours.
Había estado escribiendo por horas.

Future Tenses (Tiempos de Futuro)

Tense Structure Example & Translation
Simple S + will + V I will write a book.
Escribiré un libro.
Continuous will + be + V-ing I will be writing tomorrow.
Estaré escribiendo mañana.
Perfect will + have + V3 I will have written it by May.
Habré escrito el libro para Mayo.
Perfect Cont. will + have been + V-ing I will have been writing for a year.
Habré estado escribiendo durante un año.

Graphical Overview (Visualización Gráfica)

Continuous (Progress)

Indicates an action in progress over time.

Simple (One Point)

Indicates a specific completed or habitual point.
